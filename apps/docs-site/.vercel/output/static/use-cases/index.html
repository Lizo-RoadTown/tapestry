Use cases

Different scale.
Same thing leaks away.

Whatever you're building, context disappears between sessions and across projects. Here's where keeping it changes the outcome.

Set up a project

AI prototyping

You're moving quickly, experimenting constantly, and working across multiple repositories.

→Tapestry helps preserve decisions, surface recurring patterns, and reduce the amount of context that must be rebuilt between sessions.

Long-running projects

The longer a project lives, the harder it becomes to understand why things are the way they are.

→Tapestry captures project intelligence as work happens, making it easier to understand how the project evolved.

Research teams

Knowledge often lives in conversations, notebooks, documents, and individual contributors.

→Tapestry helps make project intelligence observable and easier to share across a team.

Agent workflows

As agent usage grows, coordination becomes harder than generation.

→Tapestry helps teams understand how agents, memory, workflows, and project structure interact over time.

Organizational learning

The most valuable lessons are often rediscovered repeatedly.

→Tapestry helps identify reusable patterns, emerging skills, and durable structures that can be applied across projects.
