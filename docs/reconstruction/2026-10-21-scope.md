## Destination system and application scopes

The toolkit: one implementation per capability (organ), scale-neutral, instantiated per layer and domain. Two assembled applications, each with its scope recorded as the procedure requires:

| Application | Scope |
|---|---|
| Personal observatory (the personal agent) | **One person across all their projects.** Private. Telemetry: that person's sessions and repos. |
| Project observatory (the Lens) | **One shared project across its contributors.** Observes only knowledge exchanges at the project's shared interfaces. Owned by the Lens roadmap; reuses toolkit mechanics without access to contributors' private context. |

Scopes are preserved when components are shared: a component moving between applications carries configuration, never the other scope's data.
